# Audio sorting guide

Classify what the audio does, not what its name claims.

## Order of decisions

1. **Structure:** loop, one-shot, phrase, or long effect.
2. **Family:** drum, instrument, voice, texture, or FX.
3. **Identity:** kick, pad, vocal phrase, riser, and so on.
4. **Character:** dark, noisy, distorted, acoustic, or synthetic.

Structure and family should be clear before choosing a narrow identity.

## Good review habits

- Listen without reading the filename.
- Pick the narrowest label supported by the audio.
- Use `_TO_REVIEW` when evidence genuinely conflicts.
- Do not turn Review into a miscellaneous folder.
- Correct wrong families before polishing flavor labels.

## Good training habits

- Approve varied examples from the same category.
- Avoid duplicates and tiny edits of one source.
- Keep evaluation samples out of training.
- Inspect which system owned the result: CLAP, memory, or fallback.
- Re-test related sounds, not only the corrected file.

Filenames, pack names, and source folders are never model evidence.
